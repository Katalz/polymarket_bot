# Strategy Tiers by Bankroll

## 1. Micro-Account ($140) -> Current Status
*   **Constraint:** Position Size ~$15 (3 tickets).
*   **Strategy:** **"Sniper All-Out" (PR4)**.
*   **Logic:** You cannot sell "a little bit". You must sell EVERYTHING to secure profit.
*   **Vibe:** Aggressive. Volatile. Fast Growth or Bust.

## 2. Grey Zone ($1,500) -> The Sweet Spot
*   **Position Size:** **$50 - $75** (3-5% risk).
*   **Ticket Count:** ~10 to 15 tickets of $5.
*   **Strategy:** **"Partial Skimming" (Hybrid)**.
*   **Logic:**
    *   Since you hold 15 tickets, you can sell **3 tickets** (20%) when up +15%.
    *   You secure competitive costs while keeping "Moonbag" exposure.
    *   You don't need to "Panic Sell All" on a small dip. You can weather volatility better because a 10% loss on $50 is still manageable (-$5).
*   **Upgrade over Micro:** You smooth out the variance curve. You stop having "Zero Profit" days because you take partial crumbs constantly.

## 3. Mid-Core ($5,000+)
*   **Position Size:** **$200+**.
*   **Strategy:** **"Defensive Hedging"**.
*   **Logic:** You can now actively BUY the opposite side (NO) to lock in arbitrage profit without the Spread killing your PnL %.
*   **Vibe:** Professional. Consistent Yield. Low Stress.
